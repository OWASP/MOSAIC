# The MOSAIC Manifesto

## Agreed principles
### Vision
- Possibilities and challenges of AI are rapidly evolving, and AI adoption is currently vastly outpacing AI security, leaving security as an afterthought
- Standards are currently fragmented, leading to uncertainty and a slowdown in innovation. A cohesive standards landscape is required to enable speed and safe adoption.
- We currently have too many standards. There is a critical need to establish consensus, coordinate, and extract best practices across the existing standards.

### Coordination: MOSAIC participants....
- remain independent
- choose collaboration over competition
- choose lightweight coordination over procedures and committees
- choose standard landscape clarity over gains for individual organizations
- choose collective coordination over 1-on-1 or no coordination
- choose open coordination

### Strive: MOSAIC participants....
- strive for being aligned on terminology and taxonomy, to prevent confusion
- strive for being open about their deliverables, plans, and roadmap, to facilitate collaboration, and prevent duplicate work
- avoid introducing yet another competing standard (referring to the famous XKCD comic) - which includes MOSAIC itself
- have a published general scope in which they operate as swimlanes.  Scope-changes are announced timely
- strive for machine readable resources, as machines will increasingly be consumers of standards and standards meta data
- choose Referring to existing work over producing work that is similar to what already exists
- don't overpromise:  remain transparent in  deliverables about the limitations of achieving security, and the reality of residual risks in AI


## Principles currently being finalized

### New members
In short: New and existing members are expected to be in scope, established, recognized, have organizational substance, active, and in agreement with the charter, including commitment to the lightweight duties of MOSAIC members.

**In scope**  
The organization publishes or maintains standards, frameworks, guidance, controls, taxonomies, or other substantive material specifically addressing the security of AI systems.

**Established**  
The organization has published and maintained relevant AI-security material for at least 12–24 months.

**Recognized / adopted**  
Its work is demonstrably used or referenced outside its own organization, e.g. by standards bodies, regulators, government agencies, industry frameworks, academic publications, or multiple independent organizations.

**Organizational substance**  
The work is maintained by an identifiable organization or community with documented governance and named maintainers/editors, rather than by a single individual.

**Active**  
Relevant material has been updated, reviewed, or actively maintained within the preceding 12 months.

**In agreement and committed**  
The organization subscribes to the charter and is committed to fulfil the lightweight coordination requirements in it (see below).

New members require 2/3 of casted votes where existing members get sufficient time to cast the vote.


### Existing members
What are the commitment levels and activity that are expected from member organizations? 
In short: Act by the principles in the charter including being transparent, have a meaningful representative, participate in discussions and share updates. All in a lightweight fashion.

**Members are actively represented**  
The organization nominates at least one representative who is directly involved in maintaining its relevant work, and can participate in taxonomy/alignment discussions or meetings. Participants should have sufficient mandate or influence within their organization to make alignment meaningful.

**Members maintain transparency**  
Members maintain the shared information about: their scope, deliverables, and roadmap - presented in the member cards, at least on a quarterly basis. They also share announcements on the MOSAIC platform when particularly relevant for the other MOSAIC members, including scope changes and initiative that may be interesting for others to join (eg. new expert groups, alignment initiatives.

**Members participate in discussions**  
Members regularly take part in discussions on the MOSAIC platform relevant to them, including alignment on terminology and insights.


### MOSAIC Scope
The topic scope for MOSAIC is: protecting CIA for AI systems, where "I" includes the integrity of intended model behaviour.  
That should not keep us from incorporating boundary aspects that are relevant, such as autonomy, transparency, excessive agency, model misuse, responsibility and accountability
