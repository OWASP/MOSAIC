MOSAIC is built around lightweight coordination: a small set of concrete activities that
improve consistency and quality across initiatives, without adding committees or process
overhead. The path below runs from the alignment work already in motion, through the
first formal milestone, and on to growing the collaboration.

## Activities already underway {#underway}

Work to get the participating initiatives aligned on shared foundations:

{{< stage when="In progress" now="true" style="margin-top:18px;" >}}
### Get alignment on the essentials

{{< checklist >}}
Shared **definitions** for foundational terms
A common **initiative template** (the "initiative card")
**Agentic identity** as a first coordinated topic, with NIST as convenor
Ad hoc **exchange of insights and information** between initiatives
{{< /checklist >}}
{{< /stage >}}

## Milestone 1 — August 1, 2026 {#milestone-1}

{{< stage when="Target · August 1, 2026" mid="true" >}}
### Finalized charter

Detail how MOSAIC and its participants work together, resulting in the MOSAIC charter —
then agree on it at an institutional level, in addition to the personal commitments
already made. This turns the founding intent into durable, shared rules of engagement.
{{< /stage >}}

## Expansion and future alignment {#expansion}

{{< stage when="Ongoing" >}}
### Grow the collaboration

{{< checklist >}}
Continue activities for alignment and exchange, and start up new ones
Onboard more organizations once growth principles are established
{{< /checklist >}}

MOSAIC is founded on an open-membership model — the intent is for collaboration to
remain accessible to any mature initiative working seriously on AI security, rather
than an exclusive club.
{{< /stage >}}
