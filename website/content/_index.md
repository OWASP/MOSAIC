{{< section >}}
{{< section-head eyebrow="What is MOSAIC" title="Coordination, not another committee." />}}
{{< prose >}}
MOSAIC — Multi-Organization Secure AI Coordination — provides a coordination structure
for initiatives working on AI security standards and guidelines, reducing inconsistencies,
duplicate work, misalignment, and gaps.

The end goal is a standards and guideline landscape that gives clear guidance for securing
and overseeing the AI systems we increasingly connect to everything and trust with our
sensitive data. The approach is deliberately lightweight: improve consistency and quality
across initiatives without adding process overhead, while every participant keeps its independence.
{{< /prose >}}
{{< /section >}}

{{< section soft >}}
{{< section-head eyebrow="The problem" title="AI adoption is outpacing AI security." subtitle="Organizations are connecting AI to everything at speed — while the guidance meant to secure it stays fragmented and hard to apply." />}}
{{< grid cols="3" >}}
{{< icon-card icon="warning" title="New, novel risks" >}}
AI systems are vulnerable to both existing and new classes of attack that require specific countermeasures — and many organizations aren't prepared.
{{< /icon-card >}}
{{< icon-card icon="grid" title="A fragmented landscape" >}}
Practitioners face multiple frameworks, overlapping recommendations, and inconsistent terminology. Each initiative has strengths — and blind spots.
{{< /icon-card >}}
{{< icon-card icon="chart" title="Speed that becomes delay" >}}
When risks materialize, projects pause and designs get revisited. What looks like speed at the start often turns into rework later.
{{< /icon-card >}}
{{< /grid >}}
{{< prose style="margin-top:28px;" >}}
Aligning standards and guidance across initiatives reduces fragmentation, improves clarity,
and gives practitioners a coherent path forward — letting organizations move fast without
losing control.
{{< /prose >}}
{{< /section >}}

{{< section >}}
{{< section-head eyebrow="April 21, 2026 · Washington DC" title="How MOSAIC began." />}}
{{< prose >}}
At the AI Security Policy Forum — organized and led by the OWASP AI Exchange with the
SANS Institute as co-host — leading standardization initiatives came together and agreed
to begin coordinating collectively on AI security. The invitation-only session ran under
Chatham House rules and was led by Rob van der Veer, founder of the AI Exchange.
{{< /prose >}}

{{< grid cols="2" style="margin-top:36px;" >}}
{{< card title="What the group decided" >}}
{{< checklist style="margin-top:16px;" >}}
Start coordinating collectively, under the name MOSAIC
Use GitHub as the shared communication platform
Keep participants fully independent
Prefer lightweight coordination over more committees
{{< /checklist >}}
{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="The shared view" >}}
<p style="margin-top:12px;">
AI's possibilities and challenges are evolving rapidly, and adoption is currently
outpacing security — leaving security as an afterthought. Standards today are fragmented,
creating uncertainty and slowing innovation. A cohesive standards landscape is required
to enable both speed and safe adoption.
</p>
